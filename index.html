Phantom Character Purge

Instantly detects and strips zero-width spaces (\u200B), non-breaking spaces, and hidden BOM codepoints that disrupt compilers and forms.

🧠

On-Device AI Heuristics

Evaluates text readability via the Flesch Reading Ease index, measures vocabulary complexity, and identifies emotional tone locally in memory.

🔒

Zero Permissions Required

Engineered without storage, tabs, or network access. Your text is processed purely in your browser and never leaves your computer.

How to Use the Extension in 3 Steps

1

Open from Toolbar

Click the Phantom AI icon in your browser toolbar to launch the compact workspace.

2

Paste or Type Your Text

Insert your content into the inspection box. Words, characters, and phantom spaces are analyzed in real time.

3

Execute One-Click Tools

Use Quick Purge to strip hidden spaces, Title Case to capitalize headings, or Copy to paste back anywhere.

Privacy & Security Commitment: This tool operates with 0 Chrome permissions. All analysis and formatting functions are executed entirely within client-side JavaScript. No user telemetry, browsing activity, or text data is collected or transmitted.
