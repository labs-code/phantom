Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Word Counter & Text Utilities ("we", "our", or "the extension") handles user information.

Summary: We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any of your data. The extension operates 100% locally on your browser.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

Word Counter & Text Utilities does not collect or request any personal identification or user data, including but not limited to:

  • Personally Identifiable Information (name, email address, physical address, IP address).
  • Browsing history, open tabs, bookmarks, or web activity.
  • Keystroke logging outside the extension popup.
  • Analytics, tracking metrics, or device identifiers.

2. How Your Input Text is Processed

Any text you paste or type into the extension is processed purely in client-side memory inside the extension popup window. It is never transmitted across the internet, never sent to remote servers, and never stored in cookies or persistent databases.

3. Permissions

This extension requires zero Chrome permissions. It does not declare storage, activeTab, cookies, webRequest, or any host access in its manifest file.

4. Third-Party Services

The extension does not use any third-party libraries, tracking scripts, advertising networks, or external APIs.

5. Changes to This Policy

If we update this policy, any changes will be reflected on this page. However, our commitment to zero data collection remains unchanged.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please open an issue in the extension's official repository or contact the developer directly.
